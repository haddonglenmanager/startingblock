Standard Operating Procedures

Daily Staff Checklists & Safety Rules

Compliance checklist standards to maintain pool deck readiness and legal standards.

Must be completed by 10:45 AM daily:

  • Deck Cleanliness: Skim pool surfaces, inspect diving wells for debris, and hose down perimeter walkways.
  • Safety Setup: Mount safety ring buoys, reach poles, and place First Aid bag at Guard Station 1.
  • Chemical Check: Run water tests, record results in the Water Quality Log, and inform the Manager of values outside CL: 1.5-3.0 / pH: 7.2-7.6.
  • Gate Check: Ensure barcode scanning console is connected and ticket parameters load correctly.

Rotations must execute every **30 minutes** to avoid staff fatigue and preserve vigilant surveillance coverage:

  • Transfer of Control: Incoming guards must stand near the tower and scan the entire zone. No guard should leave a stand unattended during transition.
  • Verification: Confirm any specific zone conditions (e.g., active lessons, kid-heavy groups, or equipment maintenance states).
  • Primary Zone Rules: Never watch cell phones, read books, or engage in lengthy conversations with members while actively scanning.

Active Rescue Protocol:

  • Activate EAP: Blow 3 short whistle blasts to clear the pool and summon secondary staff support.
  • Emergency Extraction: Rescuing guard enters water to secure victim. Supporting guards stand by to coordinate backboard extraction.
  • 911 Dispatch: Gate personnel clear the front lane and guide incoming EMS crews directly to pool access doors.
  • Post-Incident: On-duty Manager secures witnesses, records incident, and notifies the Board immediately.
