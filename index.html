Safety Guidelines

Pool Rules & Bylaws Quick Lookup

Maintain systematic safety standards across all aquatic facilities. Review compliance controls below.

Members must accompany invited guests systematically across facility perimeters. Guest access authorizations utilize clear tracking parameters:

  • Standard Weekday Entry: $8 per unit.
  • Weekend & Holiday Operational Entry: $12 per unit.
  • Long-term structural passes are clearable at the front security checkpoint.

Swimmers under 14 years old must clear baseline safety evaluation controls supervised by on-duty lifeguard personnel prior to utilizing deep lanes or boards.

Evaluation Directives:

  • Continuous transit across a 25-meter course layout without lane suspension.
  • Tread water continuously for 1.5 minutes with clear physical stability.
