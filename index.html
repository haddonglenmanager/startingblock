Safety Guidelines

Pool Rules & Bylaws Quick Lookup

Help us keep the pools clean, safe, and family-friendly. Please familiarize yourself with these key rules.

Members must accompany their guests at all times while using the facility. Guest admission requires purchase of Guest Passes:

  • Weekday Pass: $8 per guest.
  • Weekend/Holiday Pass: $12 per guest.
  • Weekly/Long-term Guest Passes are available for house guests at the front gate.
  • Members are responsible for any behavior policy violations committed by their guests.

To ensure youth safety, children under the age of 14 must pass a basic deep-end swimming test supervised by a lifeguard before using the deep lanes or diving boards.

The Deep End Test requires:

  • Swimmer must swim 25 meters continuously without touching bottom or resting.
  • Tread water for 1.5 minutes without struggling.
  • Once passed, a color-coded wrist safety band will be issued at the front gate.
  • Only one person on a diving board at any time.
  • Divers must wait until the landing area in the well is completely clear before diving.
  • Only one bounce is allowed on the board before takeoff (no repetitive high bouncing).
  • Divers must immediately swim to the nearest side ladder to exit the diving well.
  • Swimming or floating under diving boards is strictly prohibited.

The kiddie pool is reserved strictly for toddlers and children under 6 years of age. Parental supervision in this gated zone is required at all times.

Strict Infant Hygiene:

Any non-toilet trained child MUST wear an approved, snug-fitting swim diaper (reusable swim diapers with plastic covers preferred) under their swimsuit. Disposable diapers are strictly prohibited.
